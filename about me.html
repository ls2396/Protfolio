ABOUT ME

I have approximately nine years of experience in product design, having worked at three companies in China on web, mobile app, and SaaS products. In 2023, I co-founded an AI voice technology startup in the United States. I later joined AppLovin as a Data Experience Consultant, contributing to product design for Adjust and early-stage product concepts. These international experiences eventually brought me to Japan, where I hope to continue applying my expertise in a global working environment. I currently work with Low Hang Lab, contributing to the hardware and software design of Vita Ring and BirdVue, as well as the product design of Oolyra, an AI companion product. I also design enterprise solutions for the Japanese company MCD3, including an internal AI consultation and communication platform for Mitsubishi Corporation and Tachyon, an enterprise meeting-summarization product. Throughout my career, I have participated in the complete product design process—from clarifying and discussing requirements to conducting user research and data analysis, defining design strategies, and collaborating with development teams through implementation and launch. Across multiple projects, I have consistently worked to improve both product value and the overall user experience. I remain committed to a user-centered design approach, using user research and data analysis to guide decisions. By balancing usability with innovation, I aim to create greater value for both users and businesses.


Through design, I hope to make people’s lives more convenient, enjoyable, and fulfilling. To me, product design is more than visual presentation—it is a powerful way to solve real user problems and create new value. I am also deeply interested in programming and aspire to become a product designer who can bridge design and engineering. I have a solid foundation in HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, along with development experience using front-end frameworks such as React, Vue, and Next.js. This technical background enables me to design with implementation in mind, create more practical and efficient solutions, and collaborate more effectively with development teams. In the future, I aim to become a world-class Product designer. I will continue learning about emerging technologies and design trends, embrace new challenges, and explore the possibilities at the intersection of design and technology from a global perspective. Ultimately, I hope to create exceptional products that deliver meaningful value to society.
